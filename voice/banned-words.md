# Banned words

Never produce these in PRC Workforce copy. Check the exceptions at the bottom
before flagging an instance.

## The list

healing, journey, transformation, recovery, coping, feelings, processing,
breakthroughs, resilience, mindset, inner work, self-care, wellbeing,
mental health, empowerment, safe space, nurturing, balance, alignment,
authenticity, vulnerability, emotional intelligence, mindfulness, meditation,
grounding, somatic, therapy, counselling, trauma, anxiety, depression,
burnout, grey area, grey area drinker, grey drinker, wellness, inspiration,
uplifting, hope, courage, self-belief, self-esteem, gratitude, positivity,
growth, purpose, meaning, fulfilment.

## Replacements that hold the voice

| Banned | Use instead |
|---|---|
| journey, transformation | protocol, sequence, ninety days, the change |
| recovery, healing | the change, the replacement, what happens after |
| coping, processing | what the person does next, the response |
| feelings, emotional | the response, what fires, the reaction |
| resilience, mindset | capacity, consistency, the default that runs |
| wellbeing, wellness, mental health | provision, capacity, output, performance |
| burnout | sustained output loss, capacity drain |
| anxiety, depression, trauma | (do not substitute; rebuild the sentence around the pattern and its operational cost) |
| therapy, counselling | protocol, structured inputs |
| breakthrough | movement, a measurable shift |
| growth, purpose, meaning | output, capacity, the result |
| empowerment, self-belief | control of the pattern, the replacement running |
| safe space, nurturing | anonymous, private, no disclosure required |
| balance, alignment | consistency, what the day holds |
| mindfulness, meditation, grounding, somatic | (no substitute; these are not PRC mechanisms) |

## Permitted exceptions

Leave these in place. Note each one under CHANGED as a permitted exception.

1. **The fixed disclaimer.** "PRC is a behavioural protocol, not therapy or
   clinical treatment. If a clinical concern arises, stop and contact your GP
   or NHS 111." This is required copy. The word "therapy" stays.
2. **Proper nouns.** Company, team and product names owned by third parties,
   for example a broker whose division is named Employee Benefits and
   Wellbeing. Naming them is targeting, not positioning.
3. **Competitor or category labels used for internal targeting.** Describing
   the shelf a broker already sells from, in an internal document only.
   Never in participant-facing or employer-facing copy.
4. **Statutory and contractual terms.** Legal wording quoted from an Act,
   a policy or a contract.
5. **Cited titles.** The exact title of a source being referenced.

Nothing else is an exception. If in doubt, flag it.
