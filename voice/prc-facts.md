Source of truth: www.prcworkforce.com. If this file and the live site

## Naming

- The product is "PRC Workforce". Never "Professional Reality Check".
- "PRC" alone is acceptable after the first mention.
- "PRC Index" is the measure. Seven measures, self-rated 1 to 10, taken at
  Day 1, Day 30, Day 60 and Day 90.

## Delivery

- Ninety-day protocol, delivered by email, tapered cadence.
- Three pillars: Days 1-30 mapping, Days 31-60 changing the conditions,
  Days 61-90 installing the replacement.
- No app, no login, no IT involvement.
- Participants keep a private log on their own device. The only thing that
  reaches a system is a number on the dashboard.
- Reporting to the employer is quarterly, aggregate only. Not fixed Day 30,
  60 or 90 dates. Never anything with a name on it.
