Privacy notice

Written to UK GDPR Article 13. Have it reviewed by your data protection adviser before you publish it, and replace every bracketed placeholder.

Who holds your data

PRC Behavioural Operations UK Ltd, [registered office], Leek, Staffordshire Moorlands ST13, company number [00000000]. Contact: [protocol@prcworkforce.com].

What we hold

  • Your mobile number, encrypted, plus a keyed hash of it used to find your record.
  • The behaviour you named, in your own words.
  • Fourteen automaticity readings: four scores and a total, with a date.
  • A record of which weekly texts were sent.

We do not hold your name, email address, postal address, payment details, location or device identifiers. This makes the record hard to link to you. It does not make it anonymous: a mobile number is personal data, and so is a keyed hash of one. We do not describe this service as anonymous and you should not read it that way.

Why we hold it, and on what basis

  • To run the protocol you bought: performance of a contract, UK GDPR Article 6(1)(b).
  • To send the weekly audit text: your consent, recorded when you activated, which you can withdraw at any time by replying STOP.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse of the activation codes: legitimate interests, Article 6(1)(f).

We do not use your data for marketing, we do not profile you, and we do not sell or share it with anyone for their own purposes.

Who processes it for us

  • Cloudflare, Inc. — application hosting.
  • Supabase — database hosting. Configure the project in the [London / eu-west-2] region.
  • Twilio Inc. — text message delivery. Twilio necessarily sees your number in clear text in order to deliver a message to it.

Each is engaged under a written processor contract. Where these providers process data outside the UK, transfers rely on the UK International Data Transfer Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep it

Your record is deleted [twelve] months after your protocol ends, or immediately if you ask. Automaticity readings kept for research or product statistics are first separated from your number and cannot be traced back to you.

Your rights, and the fast way to use them

You can ask for a copy of your data, correction, deletion, restriction, or portability, and you can object to processing. There is no charge and we answer within one month.

  • Reply STOP to any of our texts and the messages end immediately.
  • Reply DELETE and your record and every reading are erased at once. Sending the text from your own handset is how we know it is you, so we do not have to ask you for identity documents we would then have to store.
  • Email [protocol@prcworkforce.com] for anything else.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from a third party. The pages need no consent banner because there is nothing to consent to.
