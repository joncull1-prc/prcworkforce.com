PRC Discontinuity Protocol

Day 0 baseline

This records the strength of the habit you are about to interrupt. It takes about a minute and you do it once.

Ten characters. Case and spacing do not matter.

Used only to send your weekly audit link. Stored encrypted. No name, no email, no address is collected.

The behaviour

Turn to page 1 of your ledger and write down the one behaviour you are discontinuing. Be specific: "checking my phone within five minutes of waking", not "phone use". Every question below refers to that one thing, and so does every weekly audit for the next thirteen weeks.

We do not ask you to type it here and we do not store it. It stays in your ledger, which nobody else can read. All we ever hold is your mobile number and fourteen sets of four numbers.

Automaticity, four items

Rate each statement from 1, strongly disagree, to 7, strongly agree. A high score here is expected and useful. It means the habit is strongly automatic, which is what you have come to change. The number should fall over the thirteen weeks, not rise.

Day 0 automaticity 16 of 28

Range 4 to 28. The exit threshold for this protocol is 8 or below.
